## Case 1

**case_id:** BD-CR-001
**case_title:** State vs Rahman (Unauthorized Digital Access)
**jurisdiction:** Bangladesh
**court_level:** District Court
**area_of_law:** Cyber Crime

**facts:**
The accused allegedly accessed a private company’s internal database without authorization and downloaded customer information.

**legal_issues:**

* Whether unauthorized access occurred
* Whether intent to misuse data was proven

**laws_cited:**

* Digital Security Act 2018, Section 26

**arguments_plaintiff:**
The prosecution argued that server logs and IP traces linked the accused to the unauthorized access.

**arguments_defendant:**
The defense claimed the access was accidental and no data was misused or sold.

**verdict:**
Guilty

**reasoning:**
The court found that repeated access attempts and data extraction proved intent beyond reasonable doubt.

**penalty_or_relief:**
2 years imprisonment and monetary fine

**precedent_value:** Medium
**decision_date:** 2022-08-14


## Case 2

**case_id:** BD-CV-014
**case_title:** Karim vs Noor Construction Ltd (Breach of Contract)
**jurisdiction:** Bangladesh
**court_level:** Civil Judge Court
**area_of_law:** Contract Law

**facts:**
The plaintiff hired the defendant to construct a residential building. The project was delayed by 18 months beyond the agreed deadline.

**legal_issues:**

* Whether delay constituted breach of contract
* Whether damages were justified

**laws_cited:**

* Contract Act 1872, Section 73

**arguments_plaintiff:**
The plaintiff argued financial loss due to delayed possession and rental income loss.

**arguments_defendant:**
The defendant cited material shortages and force majeure conditions.

**verdict:**
Partially in favor of plaintiff

**reasoning:**
The court accepted delay but limited damages due to external factors.

**penalty_or_relief:**
Compensation of BDT 8,00,000

**precedent_value:** Low
**decision_date:** 2021-11-03
