
## Case 3

**case_id:** BD-FM-007
**case_title:** Ayesha vs Arefin (Child Custody Dispute)
**jurisdiction:** Bangladesh
**court_level:** Family Court
**area_of_law:** Family Law

**facts:**
Following divorce, both parents sought sole custody of a minor child aged 6.

**legal_issues:**

* Best interest of the child
* Financial and emotional capability of parents

**laws_cited:**

* Guardians and Wards Act 1890

**arguments_plaintiff:**
The mother argued primary caregiving history and stable environment.

**arguments_defendant:**
The father argued superior financial capacity.

**verdict:**
Custody granted to mother

**reasoning:**
The child’s emotional wellbeing and routine were prioritized over financial capacity.

**penalty_or_relief:**
Visitation rights granted to father

**precedent_value:** Medium
**decision_date:** 2020-05-19

## Case 4

**case_id:** BD-LB-022
**case_title:** Hasan vs MegaTextiles Ltd (Wrongful Termination)
**jurisdiction:** Bangladesh
**court_level:** Labor Court
**area_of_law:** Labor Law

**facts:**
The employee was terminated without notice after 6 years of service.

**legal_issues:**

* Legality of termination
* Entitlement to compensation

**laws_cited:**

* Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, Section 26

**arguments_plaintiff:**
Termination violated mandatory notice requirements.

**arguments_defendant:**
Termination was due to company restructuring.

**verdict:**
In favor of plaintiff

**reasoning:**
Statutory notice requirements were not followed.

**penalty_or_relief:**
Reinstatement or severance pay equivalent to 6 months salary

**precedent_value:** Medium
**decision_date:** 2019-09-27

## Case 5

**case_id:** BD-PR-031
**case_title:** Chowdhury vs Alam (Land Ownership Dispute)
**jurisdiction:** Bangladesh
**court_level:** High Court Division
**area_of_law:** Property Law

**facts:**
Both parties claimed ownership over the same parcel of land based on competing deeds.

**legal_issues:**

* Validity of title deeds
* Chain of ownership

**laws_cited:**

* Transfer of Property Act 1882

**arguments_plaintiff:**
Earlier registered deed establishes ownership.

**arguments_defendant:**
Later deed executed in good faith.

**verdict:**
In favor of plaintiff

**reasoning:**
Earlier registered deed prevailed under property law principles.

**penalty_or_relief:**
Declaration of title and permanent injunction

**precedent_value:** High
**decision_date:** 2018-02-11
